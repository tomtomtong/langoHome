Video Pairs

Upload loop and transition videos in pairs, each with a time window, session prompt, and optional avatar background. Text and time changes save automatically; media uploads save when you upload. The voice app picks one pair at random within the current time window. At conversation start, the system prompt (Voice Settings) and this pair’s session prompt are combined and sent to Inworld. Its background image is used behind Uncle Tommy during the conversation.
